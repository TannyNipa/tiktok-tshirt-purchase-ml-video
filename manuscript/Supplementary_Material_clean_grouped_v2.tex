{\Large\bfseries Supplementary Material}\\[5pt]
{\large Machine Learning-Based Predictive Modelling of T-Shirt Purchase Behaviour on TikTok: From Predictors to Short-Form Video Development}
\end{center}
\vspace{4pt}

\noindent
This supplementary material provides the study instruments, hyperparameter
search spaces, complete cross-validation results, and permutation-importance
results supporting the analyses reported in the main manuscript. Questionnaire
items are presented as English renderings of the Thai instruments administered
to participants. Unless otherwise stated, questionnaire items used a five-point
Likert-type scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
